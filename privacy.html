AePDS Shop Report ("the app") helps ration shop owners in Maharashtra make sales reports from the AePDS portal. This page explains what we store and why.

What we store

  • Your district code, FPS shop number, owner name and mobile number. We store these on your phone and on our server so we can create your account and activate your shop.
  • The payment screenshot you send. We keep it on our server so we can check your payment and keep a record of it.
  • A login session, so the app knows you are signed in. Logging in on a new phone ends the session on the old one.
  • Messages you send from the "Need help?" box: your mobile number, your FPS number if you give it, and your message. We store them so we can reply to you.
  • Reports you make. These are saved on your phone.

Data from the AePDS portal

The app fetches report data from the Maharashtra AePDS portal (epos.mahafood.gov.in), using your district code and FPS number, for your shop only.

Payments

You pay once by UPI to our business account. We check each payment by hand. We never see or collect your card number or your UPI PIN. The only payment information we hold is the screenshot you send and, when available, the bank reference number we note when we approve it.

What we do with your information

  • To create your account, check your payment and activate your shop.
  • To let you log in, including on a new phone.
  • To answer your help messages and emails.

We do not sell your information. We use cloud hosting and database services to run the app, and they store data on our behalf.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your license is active, and payment records for as long as we need them for our accounts. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email the.elara.co@gmail.com with your district code and FPS number. We will reply by email. If we delete your account, your license for that shop ends.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we store or how we use it, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Email: the.elara.co@gmail.com. We help by email and through the "Need help?" box in the app only.
