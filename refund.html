एकाच दुकानासाठी दोनदा पैसे भरले, किंवा पैसे भरूनही आम्ही तुमचे दुकान सक्रिय करू शकलो नाही, तर तुम्हाला परतावा मिळेल. दुकान सक्रिय झाल्यानंतर फक्त मन बदलले म्हणून परतावा मिळत नाही. पेमेंटच्या ७ दिवसांच्या आत the.elara.co@gmail.com वर ईमेल करून मागणी करा. पूर्ण तपशील खाली इंग्रजीत आहेत.

The app costs ₹100, paid once per shop. We activate your shop by hand after we check your payment, so we handle refunds case by case, as set out below.

When we refund

  • You paid more than once for the same shop. We refund the extra payments.
  • You paid, but we could not activate your shop. For example, the payment could not be matched to your shop and we could not fix it with you.
  • You paid an amount other than ₹100 and we could not activate your shop. We refund what you paid.

When we do not refund

  • Your shop is already activated and you have changed your mind. Please use the free preview in the app before you pay.
  • You shared your login details or activation code with someone else.
  • The portal was unavailable or its data was wrong. This is outside our control, as the terms explain.

How to ask for a refund

Email the.elara.co@gmail.com within 7 days of your payment. Include your district code, your FPS number and your payment screenshot, and tell us which case above applies.

How we pay it back

We reply by email after we check your request. Approved refunds go back to the UPI account you paid from, usually within 7 working days. If a refund is approved for a shop that was activated, that shop's license ends.

Contact

Email: the.elara.co@gmail.com. We help by email and through the "Need help?" box in the app only.
